# Analysis

The analysis in this folder looks at different parts of the commercial lending lifecycle.

## Analyses

### 01 - Loan Origination
Looks at application volumes, approval rates and application trends.

### 02 - Branch Performance
Compares branches based on applications, approvals, disbursement and overdue exposure.

### 03 - Credit Risk
Looks at the relationship between credit scores and application outcomes.

### 04 - Portfolio Analysis
Looks at loan exposure, outstanding balances, loan products and customer concentration.

## Approach

The queries were written in PostgreSQL using joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs and business-focused calculations.

The goal is not only to retrieve data, but to turn lending data into information that could support business and credit decisions.
