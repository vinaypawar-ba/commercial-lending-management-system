# Credit Risk Results

## Credit Score by Application Status

| Status | Applications | Average Credit Score | Minimum Score | Maximum Score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Approved | 30 | 691.20 | 556 | 839 |
| Pending | 10 | 659.50 | 567 | 760 |
| Rejected | 10 | 684.40 | 556 | 822 |

## Business Insight

Approved applications have the highest average credit score at 691.20, while pending applications have the lowest at 659.50.

The rejected group has an average score of 684.40, which is relatively close to the approved group. This suggests that credit score alone may not explain the approval decision and should be considered alongside other risk factors.
